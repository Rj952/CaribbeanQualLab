# Privacy and research data

## What the Community Edition does
- Runs entirely in the user's browser. It makes **no network requests**, and uses no AI, analytics, cookies, fonts or scripts from other servers.
- Saves projects in the browser's IndexedDB storage **on that device only**. The author of the software never receives or sees any data.
- *Save backup* writes a `.cqrl.json` file to wherever the user chooses.

## What it does not do
- **It does not encrypt projects or backups.** Anyone with access to the browser profile or the backup file can read them.
- It cannot stop a user with legitimate access from copying data.
- Pseudonymisation replaces the words you list. It is not anonymisation: context can still identify people.

## Guidance for students and supervisors
- Follow your ethics approval and your institution's data-handling rules. They take precedence over this guidance.
- Pseudonymise transcripts **before** importing where possible, and use the built-in pseudonymiser for anything you missed.
- On shared or lab computers, save a backup to your own storage and delete the project from the device (About & licence → Delete this project) when you finish.
- Submit exports through your institution's systems (for example Moodle), not by public link.

## Data ownership
Research data, transcripts, analyses and writing created with CQRL belong to the user and to whoever holds rights in that data (participants, institutions), never to the software's author.
